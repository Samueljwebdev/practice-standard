# The Practice Standard — 60-Day Growth Sprint
**1 June → 31 July 2026 · Target: 41+ active practices · Mode: aggressive**

---

## 1. The honest funnel maths (read this first)

You're starting from ~1 practice and an empty marketplace. 41 practices in 60 days is ~**5 signups/week**. That is achievable, but **not from 64 cold emails alone** — cold email converts at low single digits. The plan below is multi-channel and front-loads two things that make the rest work:

1. **A risk-free Founding offer** (removes the "will this even work?" objection — the #1 killer on an empty marketplace).
2. **Lead volume** (scale from 64 → ~600 leads so the funnel has enough top-end).

### Funnel model (conservative)
| Channel | Volume over 60 days | → Active practices |
|---|---|---|
| Cold email (600 leads × 3% positive × 35% close) | 600 | ~6–8 |
| LinkedIn outreach (1,000 touches × 2% close) | 1,000 | ~8–12 |
| Inbound from content (reels/carousels/SEO) | — | ~6–10 |
| Referral (each founder refers ~0.5) | — | ~8–12 |
| Directory/free-tool inbound | — | ~3–5 |
| **Total** | | **31–47 → target 41 sits in range** |

**Takeaway:** No single channel gets you to 41. Run all five every week. The two highest-leverage are **LinkedIn outreach** and **referral** — give them the most attention.

---

## 2. The offer that makes 41 possible: "Founding 41"

**Positioning:** *"We're hand-picking 41 founding practices. Free for 60 days, locked-in founder pricing for life, and a Founding Member badge on your listings."*

- **Free first listing / 60-day free trial** → removes risk on an unproven marketplace.
- **Founder pricing locked for life** → £149/listing or £199/mo (below the standard £249) — creates urgency + loyalty.
- **Founding Member badge** → status + social proof you can feature in content.
- **Scarcity is real:** 41 spots, counter goes down publicly. ("31 founding spots left.")

This flips cold outreach from "buy my product" to "I'm inviting you into something." Much higher reply rates. Use `pricing-strategy` + `launch-strategy` skills to finalise the page; this needs to be **live on the site before Week 1** (a `/founding` landing page + the offer baked into every email and reel CTA).

> **Definition of "active practice" for the target:** signed up + at least one live listing (free or paid). Paid conversion is the Month-2 focus once they've seen candidate flow.

## 4. Channel playbooks (which skill runs each)

### A. Cold email — `cold-email`, `email-sequence`, `revops`
- 64 leads + 4-email sequence already written (`outreach-email1..4-drafts.md`, tracker at `outreach-tracker.csv`).
- **Week 0 job:** rewrite the email opener to lead with the **Founding 41** invite (not "we're a job board"). Subject test: *"41 founding spots — [Practice] one of them?"* vs *"verified hires, no agency fee."*
- **Deliverability:** you're on Resend (100/hr limit, domain warmed). Keep daily sends ≤40/sending-identity, spread across the day, plain-text style, one link. Do NOT blast 600 on day one — ramp.
- **NEVER cold-email dental practices** (inbound content/SEO only). Tracker is already physio/optometry/aesthetics/vet/private-medical — keep it that way as you scale.

### B. LinkedIn outreach — `social-content`, `sales-enablement`, `marketing-psychology`
- Highest-leverage channel. Search "practice manager" / "clinic director" / "practice owner" + vertical, UK.
- **20 connects/day with a note**, then a soft DM on accept. Lead with a relevant reel or the green/red-flags carousel — give value before the ask.
- Post 4–5×/week from the 52 LinkedIn drafts + reels to warm the people you're DMing.

### C. Scale lead gen — `apify-lead-generation`, `apify-ultimate-scraper`, `parallel-data-enrichment`
- You're near the Apify free cap ($4.94/$5). **Week 0 decision:** top up ~$20–30 to scrape ~500 more clinics (Google Maps → website → careers page → email). Cheapest growth money you'll spend.
- Target ~75 fresh, enriched leads/week. Enrich with named contact where possible (much higher reply).

### D. Content / distribution — `social-content`, `content-strategy`, `ad-creative`
- 22 reels + 52 LinkedIn + 52 IG posts ready. Captions in `reel-captions.md`. Calendar in `social-content-calendar.csv`.
- Mix 2 business : 1 vertical : 2 professional per week (keeps both sides of the marketplace warm — you need candidate supply to convert practices).
- Optional paid amplifier (`paid-ads`): £5–10/day boosting AgencyMaths + the Founding offer to UK practice owners once organic proves a winner.

### E. Referral loop — `referral-program`, `community-marketing`
- Build a dead-simple one: **"Refer a practice, both get an extra free month / a free featured listing."**
- Ask every founding member at the moment they get their first applicant (peak goodwill). This is where a chunk of the 41 comes from — don't skip it.

### F. Inbound / SEO — `directory-submissions`, `programmatic-seo`, `free-tool-strategy`, `lead-magnets`
- Submit to directories (kit ready: `directory-submissions-kit.md`) in Week 0–1 — compounding, low effort.
- Promote the **hiring-cost calculator** (`/tools/hiring-cost-calculator`) as a lead magnet in content + emails ("see what an agency hire really costs you").
- Programmatic SEO (city × discipline job pages) keeps generating inbound after the sprint.

## 5. Week-by-week plan

### WEEK 0 — Setup (Sun 1 Jun, ~half a day)
- [ ] Build & ship `/founding` landing page (Founding 41 offer, live spot counter).
- [ ] Rewrite Email 1 opener around the Founding invite; reconfirm tracker is dental-free.
- [ ] Top up Apify, scrape +150 leads (gets you to ~210 ready).
- [ ] Submit to directories (kit).
- [ ] Schedule Week 1 content (reels + posts).
- [ ] Set up the referral mechanic (even if manual: a tracked promo code).

### WEEKS 1–2 — Ignition (target: 8–10 practices)
- Run the weekly engine. Email batches Mon/Wed/Fri. LinkedIn 20/day Tue/Thu (push to daily if you have time).
- Content: Wk1 → AgencyMaths, GreenFlags, VetShortage, WhyIBuilt(anchor), Screening. Wk2 → EmptyChair, RedFlags, PhysioDemand, VerifiedReel(anchor), ThreeNumbers.
- Goal: first 5–8 founding signups. The moment one gets an applicant → ask for a testimonial + referral.

### WEEKS 3–4 — Momentum (target: cumulative ~20; **June checkpoint**)
- Double down on whichever channel is converting. Start featuring founding members as social proof ("Practice X just made their first verified hire").
- Content: rotate remaining reels; reuse top performers. Begin paid boost (£5–10/day) on the best business reel if organic is landing.
- **30 June checkpoint: you want ~20 active practices.** If you're under 12, the gap is almost always *volume* (scrape more, DM more) or *offer clarity* — fix before July.

### WEEKS 5–6 — Scale (target: cumulative ~32)
- Lead volume to ~100/week. Lean hard on referrals from the now-warm founding cohort.
- Start **converting free → paid**: founders who've had applicants get the "your free 60 days is working — lock founder pricing now" nudge.
- Content: case-study carousels from real practices > generic reels now.

### WEEKS 7–8 — Close (target: 41+; **31 July deadline**)
- "Founding 41 — last spots" urgency across every channel. Counter at 3, 2, 1.
- Personal closing pushes to every warm-but-unconverted conversation (LinkedIn + email).
- Final week review: lock the number, tally paid vs free, plan the post-sprint paid-conversion phase.

## 9. If you're behind (triage)
- **Behind on signups, replies OK →** offer/onboarding friction. Tighten `/founding` page (`page-cro`, `signup-flow-cro`), shorten signup, add live candidate count as proof.
- **Behind on replies →** volume or targeting. Scrape more, add named contacts, test new subject lines.
- **Replies but no closes →** the marketplace looks empty to them. Get candidate supply up (push professional reels harder) and show it ("142 verified professionals already on the platform").
- **Everything flat →** go manual: 10 hand-written, deeply personalised LinkedIn DMs/day to dream practices beats 100 templated ones.
