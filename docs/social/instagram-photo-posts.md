## Posting notes
- **Carousels** outperform single posts for saves/shares — lead with these for reach.
- Slide-1 hook text should be **large, high-contrast, lower-third** so it's legible in-feed.
- Rotate audiences: **owners (1, 2, 6, 7)** and **professionals (3, 5)** keep both sides of the marketplace warm; Post 4 is the brand/founder anchor.
- Pair each with a Story re-share 24h later (poll sticker: "Could you answer all 4 questions?" / "Do you verify registration?").
- All CTAs point to **link in bio** → set bio link to `/founding` during the sprint.
