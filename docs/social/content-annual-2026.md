# The Practice Standard — Annual Content Library (12 months)

**~87 pieces** to layer on top of the existing 52 LinkedIn + 52 Instagram posts + 22 reels. For LinkedIn / Instagram / TikTok, hand-posted by Sam. Three streams woven through the year:
- **DATE-PEGGED** — seasonal/awareness/cultural hooks (the differentiator most boards ignore)
- **INFORMATIVE** — evergreen data + how-to (publish any time the calendar is light)
- **ARTICLES** — long-form for LinkedIn/blog (12, at the end)

---

## The rules (so nothing reads cliched)

1. **Never a generic greeting.** No "Merry Christmas from the team." Every date earns its post with a number, a stance, or a useful truth tied to private-practice hiring.
2. **Respect the professional.** Clinicians are smart and tired of being marketed at. Talk like a peer, not a brochure.
3. **Take positions.** Boring is invisible. "Competitive salary' = below market" beats "we value transparency."
4. **Data > adjectives.** One real number outperforms three superlatives.
5. **Both sides, alternating.** Practices (demand) and professionals (supply) — keep both warm.
6. **On sensitive dates** (NHS birthday, religious holidays, awareness days for illness), lead with respect and never sell hard. A few are flagged **[verify date]** / **[tone-check]**.
