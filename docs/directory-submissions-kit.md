## Tips
- **Consistency matters** — same name, tagline, URL, and description everywhere helps Google connect the entity (good for brand SEO + AI citations).
- **dofollow > nofollow**, but even nofollow links drive referral traffic and discovery.
- **Save Product Hunt for a coordinated day** — it's worth doing properly with the launch-strategy playbook (queue upvotes, post at 00:01 PT, rally your network).
- **Screenshots:** use the carousel covers in `social-assets/png/` and a clean shot of `/jobs` and `/tools/hiring-cost-calculator`.
- Knock out 2–3 per day alongside the outreach — it compounds your domain authority, which lifts every SEO page you have.
